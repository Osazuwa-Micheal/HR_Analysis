# HR Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides an analytical overview of **employee demographics and performance metrics**, built using **Power BI**.  
It enables HR teams to quickly understand workforce distribution, track performance, and make informed decisions for talent management and organizational planning.  

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics
- **Total Employees:** 500  
- **Average Years of Service:** 5  
- **Average Performance Rating:** 2.36 (out of 4)  
- **Average Age:** 42  
- **Occupational Rate:** 98%  
- **Gender Distribution:** Female – 130 | Male – 370

## 🎯 Insights
- Workforce is mainly concentrated in **Operations** and **Sales & Marketing**.  
- The majority of employees are at **junior and senior officer levels**.  
- High **occupational rate (98%)** shows strong workforce utilization.  
- Balanced analysis of demographics (gender, nationality, departments) helps in **HR planning and strategy**.
